ScanStudio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20/10/2026

Developer: Nechirvan Azzo

Contact: nechirvanazzo@gmail.com

ScanStudio is a Windows desktop app for scanning documents, editing pages, exporting them to PDF, PNG, JPEG or TIFF, and recognising text with OCR. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

1. Summary

  • Your scans, images, exported files and OCR text stay on your device. ScanStudio does not upload them anywhere.
  • ScanStudio does not require an account, does not include advertising, and does not include analytics or tracking.
  • ScanStudio does not send your files, scans or usage data to the developer.
  • Purchases of ScanStudio Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store.

2. Information the app processes on your device

Scanned and imported pages. Pages you scan or import are stored temporarily in your Windows temporary folder (%TEMP%\ScanStudio\Sessions). Pages are deleted when you remove them, and the session folder is deleted when you close the app. If the app was closed unexpectedly, leftover session folders are removed the next time it starts.

Exported files. Files you export (PDF, PNG, JPEG, TIFF) are saved to the location you choose. The app does not keep copies of them.

OCR text. Text recognition runs entirely on your device using the open-source Tesseract engine. Recognised text is kept in the app while you work on it and is not sent anywhere.

Log files. ScanStudio writes local log files to help diagnose problems. Logs may include the app version, Windows version, memory usage, scanner names, file paths, and error messages. Logs are kept for up to 14 days, with a maximum of 10 MB per file. They remain on your device. You can open the log folder from within the app, and you can delete the files at any time.

Scanner access. To find and use scanners, ScanStudio uses the Windows Image Acquisition (WIA) service built into Windows. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or other personal data.

3. Purchases and licences

ScanStudio Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. When you use the Upgrade option, the app asks the Microsoft Store whether your account owns the Pro add-on. The app receives only the licence status (free, trial, subscription or lifetime). Payment details and purchase records are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive your payment information.

4. Information we do not collect

ScanStudio does not collect or transmit:

  • your name, email address or account details,
  • the contents of your scans, images, PDFs or OCR text,
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage statistics,
  • location data.

5. Third-party components

ScanStudio uses open-source libraries for PDF creation, OCR and logging. These libraries run locally and do not send data over the internet. OCR language data files (for example eng.traineddata) are installed by you and stay on your device.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Temporary page files: deleted when pages are removed or when the session ends.
  • Log files: kept for up to 14 days, then removed automatically.
  • Exported files: stored wherever you save them. You control them.

To remove all ScanStudio data from your device, uninstall the app and delete the ScanStudio folder in your local application data.

7. Children

ScanStudio does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with an updated effective date. If a future version of ScanStudio adds features that send data off the device, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for your consent.
